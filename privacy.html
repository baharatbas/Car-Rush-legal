Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

EnglishTürkçe

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Durak Rush ("the app", "we") handles information when you play the game on iOS or Android. We built the game to work without accounts and without sending your game data to our servers.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, e-mail address, phone number, or any other contact details.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data. The app does not request these permissions.
  • The app has no in-app purchases. Coins and stars are earned only by playing and have no real-world value.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app stores the following only on your device (in a local database): your current level, stars, coins, purchased or equipped outfits, tutorial progress, and settings (sound, music, vibration, color-blind mode, performance mode, language). This data never leaves your device through our app and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier (e.g. IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This processing is carried out by Google under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The app requests non-personalized ads only. On iOS, if the system asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), you can decline and the app keeps working normally. You can also limit ad personalization at any time: on iOS in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and Apple Advertising; on Android in Settings › Google › Ads.

4. Children

Durak Rush is a general-audience puzzle game and contains no chat, social features, or user-generated content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Ads are requested in non-personalized mode. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us (see section 9) and we will help.

5. Legal bases and your rights

Because we do not receive or store personal data about you on our own servers, there is generally no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. Data processed by Google for advertising is controlled by Google; you can exercise your rights over it through Google's tools linked above. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Türkiye (KVKK), you also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app or clear its storage. Uninstalling the app removes all data we store.

7. Security

Local data is kept in the app's private storage area, which is protected by your device's operating system. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of data to the minimum needed for the game to work.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature or change an ad provider. The "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be published at this address.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: please open an issue at https://github.com/baharatbas/Car-Rush-legal/issues. Please do not post personal information in a public issue.
